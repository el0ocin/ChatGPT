# Service Workflow

This is the lean version of the delivery process.

## Standard workflow
1. Intake received
2. Item triaged
3. AI draft generated
4. Human QC completed
5. Client access received
6. Listing posted
7. URLs saved in Notion
8. Buyer scripts sent to client
9. 48-hour review completed
10. Result captured in `Proof / Results`

## Time target per item
- 5 minutes: review intake and photo folder
- 10 minutes: run the prompt and get the first draft
- 10 minutes: pricing review and human QC
- 10 minutes: publish to Facebook Marketplace + KSL
- 5 minutes: save links, send buyer scripts, and update Notion

Target: under 45 minutes per item once intake is clean.

## Intake standard
Each item should arrive with:
- 5 to 12 clear photos
- 2 to 5 seller notes
- city or region
- pickup landmark or neighborhood
- urgency
- lowest acceptable price
- allowed platforms
- account access method

If any of those are missing, the job is not clean yet.

## Item triage rule
Quick triage before running AI:
- is this a quick sale or max profit candidate?
- does the item have broad local demand?
- does the item need only Facebook + KSL, or is one add-on platform worth it?
- is the photo set strong enough to trust as the source of truth?

## AI draft standard
The AI output must produce only:
- item ID and condition
- strategy label
- list price + floor price
- one title and one short description per platform
- photo cover choice and image order
- buyer replies
- next best move

If the output turns into a long report, trim it.

## Human QC standard
Before posting, verify:
- every factual claim is visible or clearly marked uncertain
- the lead sentence contains the strongest differentiator
- the price matches the urgency
- the copy sounds human, not corporate
- the pickup line is safe and local

## Posting rule
- Default platforms: Facebook Marketplace + KSL
- Only add OfferUp or Nextdoor if the item or local audience clearly benefits
- Save URLs immediately after posting
- Update the `Items` and `Jobs` databases the same session

## 48-hour repricing rule
If the item is still live after 48 hours:
- check views and inquiries first
- if views are low, improve title and lead sentence before slashing the price
- if views are solid but buyers stall, make a small price move
- default repricing move: 5% to 8%, not a panic drop

## Proof capture rule
Once sold, capture:
- original list price
- revised list price if changed
- final sold price
- time to sale
- platforms used
- what changed that helped
- screenshot or listing link if available

This turns delivery into future marketing proof.
