# Outreach Scripts

Use these to get the first paying clients fast.

## Warm DM
`Hey, I am testing a local service where I turn a pile of item photos and notes into live Facebook Marketplace and KSL listings for people who keep putting it off. If you have stuff to sell, I can handle the pricing, copy, photo order, and posting. I am opening a few beta spots at $149 for up to 3 items. Want details?`

## Slightly more casual version
`If you have items you want gone but have not actually listed yet, I can do the annoying part for you. Send me the photos and a few notes, and I will turn them into live Marketplace + KSL listings.`

## Facebook group post
`Testing a local resale service for anyone in Utah County who has stuff to sell but keeps putting it off. I take your item photos and notes, write the listings, price them realistically, and post them to Facebook Marketplace + KSL. Beta pricing is $149 for up to 3 items. Message me if you want one of the first spots.`

## Follow-up after interest
`Perfect. The beta offer is $149 for up to 3 items posted to Facebook Marketplace + KSL within 48 hours after I get clean photos, notes, and account access. If you want to do it, I will send the intake template next.`

## After they ask what you need
`I need the item photos, a few notes about each item, your city or pickup area, your lowest acceptable price, and the platforms you want me to use. I also have a simple intake template that makes this fast.`

## Ask for testimonial after a win
`Would you be open to giving me a short testimonial about how the process felt and whether it helped you finally get the item listed and sold?`

## Ask for referral
`If you know anyone else with clutter, moving piles, or items they keep meaning to sell, I would love an intro.`

## One-line positioning statement
`I help overwhelmed local sellers turn photo folders into live listings fast.`
