# Marketplace Listing Sprint Offer

## Core positioning
You are not selling "AI research."

You are selling this outcome:

`Send me your item photos and a few notes, and I'll turn them into live local listings fast.`

## Who this is for
- overwhelmed local sellers
- moms clearing clutter
- people moving or downsizing
- people with photo folders and good intentions who never actually post
- anyone who wants the listings live without learning a bunch of tools

## Who this is not for
- high-volume full-time flippers who want complex automation first
- national e-commerce shipping businesses
- clients who want heavy photo editing or brand design
- clients who need dozens of marketplace accounts managed at once

## Beta offer
- Name: `Marketplace Listing Sprint`
- Price: `$149`
- Scope: up to 3 items
- Platforms included: Facebook Marketplace + KSL
- Turnaround: within 48 hours after clean intake and access
- Add-on: `+$39` for OfferUp or Nextdoor cross-posting when strategically useful

## What the client gets per item
- strategy label: quick sale or max profit
- list price and floor price
- Facebook title and short description
- KSL title and short description
- cover photo pick and image order
- buyer reply script pack
- live listing links after posting
- 48-hour repricing note

## What you do not include in v1
- long market reports
- custom dashboards
- photo retouching
- shipping workflows
- eBay, Mercari, and Poshmark by default
- advanced automations

## Delivery promise
`I will take your photos and notes, write the listing copy, price it realistically, choose the best image order, and get it live on Facebook Marketplace and KSL.`

## How to talk about the service
Short version:

`I help overwhelmed people turn piles of stuff and photo folders into live local listings fast.`

Longer version:

`If you have items to sell but keep putting it off, I can take your photos and notes, turn them into ready-to-post Marketplace and KSL listings, and publish them for you.`

## Pricing notes
- Do not discount below $149 just because the process feels easy to you.
- The pain you are removing is delay, clutter, indecision, and posting avoidance.
- The first 3 clients should be sold as beta spots, not permanent low pricing.

## Upgrade path after first proof
- `Sprint Mini`: 1 item for a lower entry price
- `Sprint Standard`: 3 items for $149
- `Sprint Declutter`: 10-item package with staged rollout
- `Repricing Rescue`: fix stale listings that are not converting

## Sales guardrails
- Promise speed and clarity, not guaranteed sale price.
- Promise honest listings and smart local positioning, not miracles.
- Sell the live-posting outcome, not the software stack behind it.
