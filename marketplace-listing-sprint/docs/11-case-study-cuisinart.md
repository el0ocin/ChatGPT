# Case Study: Cuisinart ICE-100

This is the first proof asset for `Marketplace Listing Sprint`.

## Snapshot
- Item: Cuisinart ICE-100 Compressor Ice Cream & Gelato Maker
- Area: Lehi, Utah
- Platforms used: Facebook Marketplace and KSL
- Revised listing price shown publicly: $225

## Starting problem
Based on your notes:
- the item had already been listed about 7 days earlier
- the first version was priced too high
- the first photo set was good, but not strong enough to stop the scroll
- the listing did not convert fast enough

## What changed on the revised version
- stronger cover photo showing the full bundle
- tighter value proposition: `no bowl freezing required`
- shorter, cleaner listing copy
- clear local pickup line
- more realistic local pricing
- cross-posting to the main local platforms

## Public proof points captured on March 26, 2026
From the KSL listing snapshot:
- title: `Cuisinart ICE-100 Compressor Ice Cream & Gelato Maker`
- price: `$225`
- condition: `Used - Excellent, like new`
- page views at snapshot: `21`
- favorites at snapshot: `0`
- photo count visible: `7`

From the Facebook screenshot:
- same item and same local positioning
- strong cover image with the machine, manual, paddles, recipe book, and containers visible
- concise description focused on the differentiator and included extras

## Reported outcome
You reported that the revised listing sold in under 24 hours after the repost.

## What this proves
- pricing and presentation mattered more than extra research layers
- the cover image and first two lines did a lot of the work
- a realistic local price plus a clear differentiator beat a slower, higher-priced first pass

## How to tell the story in marketing
Short version:

`One kitchen appliance sat after an overpriced first pass. After a better cover image, tighter local copy, and a more realistic price, the revised listing sold in under 24 hours.`

Longer version:

`A premium ice cream maker was already listed once, but the first version was priced too high and did not present the bundle strongly enough. On the revised version, the cover photo showed the full setup, the listing led with the biggest differentiator, the copy got shorter, and the price fit the local market better. The item sold in under 24 hours after the repost.`
