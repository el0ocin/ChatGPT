# Marketplace Listing Sprint

This folder turns your resale process into a sellable, service-first operating kit.

The goal is simple:
- help overwhelmed local sellers go from photo pile to live listings fast
- keep Notion as the internal control tower, not the product
- remove the extra AI ceremony that slows delivery down

## What this kit includes
- a clear beta offer with pricing and scope
- import-ready Notion database templates
- one streamlined master prompt
- delivery checklists for pricing, QC, posting, and buyer replies
- security rules for temporary credentials
- outreach scripts to land the first clients
- a case study based on the Cuisinart listing win

## Quick start
1. Open [01-offer-and-pricing.md](C:/Users/el0ci/OneDrive/Documents/ChatGPT/marketplace-listing-sprint/docs/01-offer-and-pricing.md) and memorize the core promise.
2. Import the CSV files in [templates/notion](C:/Users/el0ci/OneDrive/Documents/ChatGPT/marketplace-listing-sprint/templates/notion) into your fresh Notion account. If Notion asks for a title property, choose the first column, then delete the sample row after import.
3. Follow [02-notion-setup.md](C:/Users/el0ci/OneDrive/Documents/ChatGPT/marketplace-listing-sprint/docs/02-notion-setup.md) to create the basic relations and views.
4. Use [intake-template.md](C:/Users/el0ci/OneDrive/Documents/ChatGPT/marketplace-listing-sprint/templates/client/intake-template.md) to collect the client info.
5. Paste the prompt from [04-master-prompt.md](C:/Users/el0ci/OneDrive/Documents/ChatGPT/marketplace-listing-sprint/docs/04-master-prompt.md) into ChatGPT for each item.
6. Run the output through the pricing, QC, and posting checklists before you publish.
7. Capture results in `Proof / Results` and build more case studies as items sell.

## Beta offer snapshot
- Offer name: `Marketplace Listing Sprint`
- Price: `$149` for up to 3 items posted to Facebook Marketplace + KSL within 48 hours
- Add-on: `+$39` for OfferUp or Nextdoor when it actually helps
- Target buyer: overwhelmed local sellers who want the listings live, not another system

## Ground rules
- No long reports unless they directly help the sale.
- No fake claims, over-polished AI tone, or photo editing promises.
- No storing client credentials in Notion.
- Default stack: Facebook Marketplace + KSL first, local pickup first.
